The Process

From zero to court-admissible in four steps

No branch visits. No paper notarisation. No weeks-long procurement cycles.

1

Enrol your AECE token

Your signatory completes AECE identity verification online in under 7 minutes, receiving a Class 3 cryptographic token tied to their legal identity. No branch visit, no paper notarisation — the accreditation authority processes credentials digitally through SigMa's certified onboarding pipeline.

2

Upload and route documents

Drag any PDF contract into SigMa's dashboard or trigger upload via the REST API directly from your ERP. Define signing order — sequential for board resolutions, parallel for multi-party supplier agreements — and dispatch in a single click.

3

Collect qualified signatures instantly

Each signatory receives a secure, time-limited signing link and authenticates with their AECE token via OTP or hardware key. SigMa applies the Class 3 qualified signature cryptographically, timestamped against Algeria's national time authority.

4

Receive court-admissible evidence pack

At completion SigMa automatically generates a tamper-evident audit report containing IP logs, identity assertions, certificate chain, and document hash — packaged as a single PDF admissible as evidence under Algerian procedural law without additional expert testimony.
